The one-paragraph setup

Toast added a record 30,000 net locations in 2025 (vs 28k '24, 27k '23, 22k '22) and guided to another record in 2026; Q1'26 came in at ~7,000 vs ~6,000 a year ago. The core US SMB engine is mature-but-steady (~20% share, management says core adds stay "in a similar range"), so the incremental net-adds story is now enterprise rollouts (Applebee's, Firehouse Subs, Papa Murphy's, Topgolf, TGI Fridays), international (4 countries) and F&B retail — a bucket that crossed 10k locations and ~$100M ARR in 2025 and doubled y/y. The industry backdrop is unusually supportive of the funnel: restaurant business applications are at record highs (+13% TTM) while net establishment growth is only +1.6% — Toast's adds are share-capture of gross openings plus legacy-POS conversions, not a bet on industry expansion. Watch-items for the short side: GPV/location −1% y/y, enterprise ARPU dilution, soft restaurant traffic (8 straight months of net decline), chain closure wave (Wendy's, Pizza Hut, Papa John's), and a 150bps hardware-cost headwind.

▲ Bull case (long)

  • Net-adds momentum: every quarter of 2025 beat its year-ago comp; 2026 guided to a 4th straight record. Q1'26 +7k vs +6k LY annualizes to ~33k.
  • Leading indicator inflecting: NAICS-72 business applications +12% YTD, record monthly highs Jan–May '26 — new restaurants overwhelmingly pick cloud POS, where Toast claims rising win rates vs every major competitor.
  • Enterprise flywheel just starting: two largest-ever signings (Applebee's, Firehouse) still rolling out; "booked more locations in Q1'26 than total customers in 2023"; drive-thru product opens ~140k-location QSR TAM.
  • Competition stumbling: Fiserv/Clover guidance cut (Oct '25, −40% day), Olo taken private, NCR/Micros legacy base (~45% of market) is the share-donor pool.
  • Monetization compounding: ARR/location $10.0k→$12.6k since '21; take rate crossed 1% of GPV; FY26 FCF consensus ~$714M.

▼ Bear case (short)

  • Core is flat: mgmt admits core SMB adds are "in the same range" — all growth now from lower-ARPU new TAMs; UBS flags enterprise as dilutive to front-book ARPU; GPV/location −1% y/y.
  • End-market deteriorating: operator traffic down 8 straight months; 2026 closure wave (Wendy's ~300+, Pizza Hut 250, Papa John's 200); franchisee distress = churn risk in the base.
  • Enterprise math is small & shrinking hosts: identifiable chain deals ≈ 7–8k locations (~4–5% of base); flagship Applebee's is net-closing (−35/yr); QSR mega-chains locked up by PAR (Burger King), Qu (GoTo Foods, Jack in the Box), NCR (Wendy's).
  • Decel + margin pressure: ARR growth 26% and fading ~4-5pts/yr; 150bps memory-chip hardware headwind; Mizuho cut PT to $38 on margins (May '26).
  • Law of large numbers: 30k adds on 731k US establishments growing +11.5k/yr — Toast already captures >2.5x industry net growth; every incremental point of share is harder.

Churn & competitive map

  • LOST Jamba (GoTo Foods) — the only documented enterprise loss. Toast declined renewal in 2020; transition completed ~2022–23; GoTo Foods standardized ~1,500 locations across 7 brands on Qu. Jamba units ~flat/−0.4%.
  • Locked-up QSR tier (not Toast's): Burger King → PAR (exclusive, Oct '23); Jack in the Box & Dave's Hot Chicken → Qu; Wendy's → NCR Aloha. Firehouse Subs is notable precisely because parent RBI's Burger King is PAR-exclusive.
  • NCR Voyix claims #1 in new restaurant deployments (Datos, Dec '25); SpotOn undercutting on price; Square still the broad-SMB share leader. Toast counter: drive-thru product launched Apr '26 ("opens ~140k locations").
  • Retention disclosure: no churn rate published; SaaS NRR 109% (2025); enterprise churn described only qualitatively as "low".

How much do enterprise wins actually move net adds?

  • Identifiable named-chain deals sum to roughly 7–8k locations ≈ 4–5% of the 171k base [E]. The combined enterprise + international + retail bucket crossed 10k locations (~6–7%) and ~$100M ARR (~5%) in 2025, doubling y/y.
  • Mgmt (Q4'25): 2025's record was core "in the same range" + new TAMs providing the increment — i.e., ~28k core + ~2k new-TAM. Same pattern guided for 2026.
  • In-flight rollouts that land in 2026 net adds: Applebee's (~1.5k), Firehouse (~1.3k), Papa Murphy's (~1.0k), Topgolf (full by end Q2'26), TGI Fridays, Hungry Howie's (~0.5k), Teriyaki Madness (0.2k) → ~3–4k cumulative [E].
  • Hosts' organic growth skews negative at the biggest deal (Applebee's −35/yr) but positive at Nothing Bundt Cakes (+100/yr), Potbelly (+31 in '25, 2,000-shop target under RaceTrac), and the hotel systems (Hilton NUG +6.7%, Marriott +4.3%).

Reading it for TOST

  • The funnel is national and accelerating: 316k restaurant-sector applications TTM (+13.4%), record monthly highs all of 2026 YTD. This is the single best public leading indicator for Toast's gross adds.
  • Sun-belt application growth (TX +17%, NC +21%, AZ +20%, GA +13%, TN +23%) keeps the new-restaurant mix in markets where cloud POS is default and Toast claims share gains in "all top-100 SMB markets."
  • Counter-narrative check: realized establishment counts show CA fastest among mega-states (+3.6%) and TN shrinking (−2.4%) — formation hype ≠ net openings; conversions of the existing 731k base matter more to Toast than net industry growth.
  • Shrinking-state risk is small: states with negative establishment growth (MT, TN, OR, ID, NY, IL, OH...) sum to a tiny net decline; churn pressure on Toast's base from closures is diffuse, concentrated instead in legacy-POS QSR franchise systems (Wendy's/Pizza Hut/Papa John's 2026 closures).
